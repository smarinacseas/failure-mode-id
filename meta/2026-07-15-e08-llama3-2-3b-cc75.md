# E08-llama3-2-3b-cc75 · 2026-07-15 · `caffeinate -is uv run python main.py all --experiment E08-llama3-2-3b-cc75 --limit 75 --candidates llama-3b=meta-llama/llama-3.2-3b-instruct --judges opus48=anthropic/claude-opus-4.8,gemini-pro=google/gemini-3.1-pro-preview,gpt56sol=openai/gpt-5.6-sol --tiebreaker opus48 --reasoning off --temperature 0.6 --max-tokens 1536 --provider-quant bf16,fp16 --seed 20260715`

Baseline failure-mode census of **Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct** on the Complex
Constraints benchmark (CC-75: 75 multi-constraint prompts, 1,559 pass/fail
criteria), graded by a **three-provider judge panel** (Opus 4.8 · Gemini 3.1
Pro · GPT-5.6-sol) under the §0.3 consensus policy that replaces E07's
contaminated dual-judge design. The goal is to characterize *this* subject's
failure mix — it is a different model family and scale than E07's Qwen3.5
ladder, so its failure modes were unknown going in — and to hand a
Pareto to Gate E→T that binds T01's `CAUSE_A`/`CAUSE_B`. Success = a clean,
panel-verified census whose top causes map onto the coverage/precision
archetypes, plus a standalone cross-family replication finding. This is the
E-series' first cross-family census and the last gate before the T-series.

## TL;DR

- **The constraint-satisfaction gap is extreme.** Llama-3.2-3B passes
  **42.78%** of individual criteria but **0.0%** of whole prompts (0/75) — it
  can satisfy roughly two of every five requirements, and *zero* of 75
  multi-constraint prompts in full.
- **Failure Pareto (n=892 failed criteria):** `execution_slip` **34.8%** ·
  `constraint_unaddressed` **28.8%** · `constraint_misread` **12.4%** ·
  `degenerate_output` **12.0%** · `input_misread` **9.2%**. The top two
  (precision + coverage, **63.6%** combined) are the Gate E→T binding.
- **The panel redesign worked.** **Zero abstentions** across all 3 judges ×
  1,559 criteria (E07's Fable refused ~15%), Fleiss **κ = 0.871**, 0 excluded
  criteria, completeness gate satisfied on all 1,559 cells. The verdict basis
  is trustworthy.
- **Cross-family replication holds directionally:** the head of the Pareto —
  {execution_slip, constraint-tracking, constraint_misread} — is the *same set*
  as E07's Qwen ladder, across a different family, scale, and reasoning mode.
  Internal order and exact shares differ (taxonomy changed), so it is a
  directional, not like-for-like, comparison.
- **Two load-bearing caveats:** (1) 95% of the Pareto's labels came from a
  single classifier (`claude-fable-5`); the human hand-check is **deferred**.
  (2) At the tight 1,536-token answer budget, 12/75 responses hit a non-stop
  finish — `degenerate_output` here is truncation, not repetition (only **1**
  mechanical loop fired), and is plausibly budget-inflated.

| 2 | `constraint_unaddressed` | 257 | **28.8%** | **coverage** → `CAUSE_A` |
| 3 | `constraint_misread` | 111 | 12.4% | comprehension |
| 4 | `degenerate_output` | 107 | 12.0% | decode/termination |
| 5 | `input_misread` | 82 | 9.2% | comprehension |
| 6 | `constraint_sacrificed` | 20 | 2.2% | conflict-arbitration |
| 7 | `other` | 4 | 0.4% | — |
| 8 | `judge_suspect` | 1 | 0.1% | *excluded from interpretation* |

**`execution_slip` (right plan, wrong rendering: arithmetic/count errors,
format markup omitted, per-item rules replaced by one template, values never
self-checked) leads at 34.8%.** `constraint_unaddressed` (28.8%) is the
**reasoning-off collapse** of the coverage family — with no reasoning trace to
inspect (all 75 traces `absent`), the classifier cannot separate
"never extracted" from "tracked then dropped from the answer," so E07's
`constraint_never_surfaced` + `constraint_dropped` merge into one label. It is
the direct analog of E07's `constraint_dropped`.

**Broken out (plan §E8.2.1), where counts permit:**

- `execution_slip` (310): concentrated in **Logistics 162** and **Data/Math
  114**; by instruction type **Multistep 103** / Negative 94. The rendering
  errors live in the arithmetic- and schedule-heavy prompts.
- `constraint_unaddressed` (257): **Logistics 97**, **Data/Math 87**,
  Professional 47; **Conditional 94** is the top instruction type — conditional
  triggers are the most-frequently-skipped requirement class.
- `degenerate_output` (107): **Data/Math 44**, **Logistics 44**; **Implicit
  39**. These are the longest, most structured outputs — the ones that hit the
  token cap (see caveat).
- `constraint_misread` (111) and `input_misread` (82) both concentrate in
  Logistics + Data/Math — the prompts with dense source material (rosters,
  tables) to misparse.

## Judge-panel health — the §0.3 redesign, validated

| metric | value |
| --- | --- |
| Fleiss κ (3 judges, over non-abstained cells) | **0.8714** (almost-perfect) |
| abstentions | **0 / 0 / 0** (opus48 / gemini-pro / gpt56sol) — 0 of 4,677 judge×criterion cells |
| excluded criteria (EXCLUDE verdict) | **0** |
| completeness gate (≥2 non-abstaining verdicts) | **satisfied on all 1,559 cells** (0 incomplete) |
| pairwise agreement | opus↔gemini 94.2% · opus↔gpt 93.7% · gemini↔gpt 93.2% |
| agreement with consensus | opus 97.4% · gemini 96.9% · gpt 96.3% |

This is the headline methodological result. **E07's dual-judge design was
contaminated by Fable refusing ~15% of cells** (33/225 in E07, same rate as
E05); those refusals had to be handled as a reliability hole. The three-provider
panel with an explicit ABSTAIN/tie-break/EXCLUDE protocol produced **zero
abstentions** and near-perfect agreement — so no criterion's verdict rests on a
refusal or a coin-flip tie. The panel is the reliability control that the
plan (§0.3) argued should *substitute* for a manual verdict hand-check at this
stage, and the κ / 0-abstention numbers are the evidence that it did its job.

The reasoning-off prediction held: **1 loop vs E07's 19 for Qwen-9b.** Repetition
looping is a thinking-channel behavior; with reasoning disabled it nearly
vanishes. **But `degenerate_output` was still the #4 failure cause (107
criteria) — so in E08 it is *not* repetition.** The 13 prompts carrying all 107
degenerate labels are 11/13 `finish_reason=length`: this is **run-to-cap
truncation at the 1,536-token budget**, a mechanically distinct mode from E07's
loops (the taxonomy-v2 split E07 flagged). The `experiments/E08/ablation-degenerate/`
harness is built to test temperature-sensitivity of this mode at t=0.2/0.6, but
its arms have **not** yet been generated (baseline extract only).

## Cross-family comparison vs E07's Qwen ladder (plan §E8.2.2)

A standalone finding regardless of T01's outcome: **does the small-model failure
mix replicate across families?** Directionally, yes — with explicit non-parity
caveats (E08 changes candidate, reasoning, *and* taxonomy simultaneously, so
this is cross-model directional, never like-for-like).

| | E07 (Qwen3.5, reasoning on) | E08 (Llama-3.2-3B, reasoning off) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| failure density | ~4.2 labels/cell (814 / 192) | **~11.9 labels/cell (892 / 75)** |
| #1 cause | `execution_slip` 23.8% [17.5, 31.0] | `execution_slip` **34.8%** |
| coverage family | `dropped`+`overridden`+`never_surfaced` ≈ 35.0% | `constraint_unaddressed` **28.8%** |
| `constraint_misread` | 16.6% [12.5, 21.2] | **12.4%** |
| `degenerate_output` | 3.9% (32, ≈all Qwen-9b) | **12.0%** (107) |
| `judge_suspect` | 9 | 1 |

**Stable head-set, unstable internal order.** {execution_slip, constraint-tracking,
constraint_misread} is the top-3 set in both families. The #1/#2 ordering flips
depending on how the merged coverage label maps back onto E07's three
categories. `execution_slip` leads in both — the strongest cross-family signal.
The `degenerate_output` surge (3.9% → 12.0%) reproduces the pattern that the
*smallest* model carries this mode, and notably it persists **with reasoning
disabled**, which directionally weakens the "reasoning-trace artifact"
hypothesis for degeneracy — though the model change and the 1,536-token cap both
confound it.

## Experimental flaws, biases, and limitations

### Classifier provenance + deferred hand-check (the load-bearing caveat)
The root-cause Pareto — the input to Gate E→T — is **95.2% labeled by a single
classifier**: `claude-fable-5` diagnosed 72/75 prompt-records (849/892 labels),
with `claude-opus-4-8` as chain fallback on 3 records (43 labels). Per §0.3's
binding rationale the **40-row manual label hand-check is deferred**: the
3-judge consensus/κ/ABSTAIN protocol is a stronger *verdict-reliability* control
than a 40-row human spot-check, but it does **not** validate *cause labels*,
which remain a separate, human-unvalidated concern. Moreover the taxonomy
changed since E07 (coverage-family merge), so E07's frozen classifier-prompt SHA
no longer describes the classifier in use — making eventual human scoring *more*
urgent, not less. **Forward pointer:** if T01's (Part II) primary result is null
or ambiguous, the hand-check is the recommended first follow-up, per T1.6
threats-to-validity — not a blocking prerequisite now.

### Sample-size + selection effects
Single candidate, single 75-prompt population census (limit=75, no sampling → no
sampling error vs the benchmark). No within-family ladder this round, so scaling
claims are cross-report only. The four singleton use-cases (Health/Technical/
Creative, n=1) are excluded from all interpretation. Each cell measured once
(k=1) — generation stochasticity is unmeasured here (the ablation's t06 arm
would give a same-config replication draw once run).

### Decoding-mode caveat (`max_tokens=1536`)
The tight answer budget truncated **12/75 responses** (non-stop finish, mostly
`length`). `degenerate_output` (12.0% of failures) is concentrated in the 13
longest-output prompts and is plausibly **budget-inflated**: criteria whose
content was never reached count as failures. This does not undermine the top-two
causes (`execution_slip` + `constraint_unaddressed` are about wrong/omitted
content, not truncation), but it means the census characterizes
"Llama-3.2-3B at a 1,536-token answer budget," and a higher-budget re-run is the
clean way to bound the truncation contribution.

### Judge biases
Self-preference is structurally absent: the candidate is Meta-family, no judge
is (family_overlap=false for all three). Verbosity/position/format biases are
uncontrolled but bounded by the near-perfect cross-provider agreement (κ=0.871)
— three independent providers rarely co-hallucinate the same verdict. Human
verdict validation remains the open gate (60-row sample staged in
`judge_validation.json`, unscored).

### Provider-side variance
The bf16/fp16 pin routed to a single provider (Parasail, quantization reported
`null` = native precision). Provider drift is pinned on precision but not on
identity; time-of-day/load uncontrolled. No timeouts or malformed responses
observed in the census pass.

### Cost / timing
Wall-clock ≈ 1 h (single-candidate pass; 3-judge panel graded concurrently on
the OpenRouter pool). **Token/dollar spend is not instrumented** (the usage
sidecar remains a program-wide TODO, inherited from E07); the pre-run estimate
was ~$30–60 for the 3-judge census.
