Baseline failure-mode census of **Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct** on the Complex
Constraints benchmark (CC-75: 75 multi-constraint prompts, 1,559 pass/fail
criteria), graded by a **three-provider judge panel** (Opus 4.8 · Gemini 3.1
Pro · GPT-5.6-sol) under the §0.3 consensus policy that replaces E07's
contaminated dual-judge design. The goal is to characterize *this* subject's
failure mix — a different model family and scale than E07's Qwen3.5 ladder — and
to hand a Pareto to Gate E→T that binds T01's `CAUSE_A`/`CAUSE_B`. Success = a
clean, panel-verified census whose top causes map onto the coverage/precision
archetypes, plus a standalone cross-family replication finding. This is the
E-series' first cross-family census and the last gate before the T-series.

> **Numbers below are post-truncation-repair.** The census first ran at
> max_tokens=1536 (§0.2), which truncated 12/75 answers; those were re-drawn at
> 8192 (9 finish cleanly, 3 are genuinely degenerate) — see
> [Truncation repair](#truncation-repair-2026-07-15). Pre-repair figures are
> shown alongside where they moved.

## TL;DR

- **The constraint-satisfaction gap is extreme.** Llama-3.2-3B passes
  **43.43%** of individual criteria but **0.0%** of whole prompts (0/75) — even
  after fixing truncation so every prompt had room to finish, it lands *zero* of
  75 multi-constraint prompts in full.
- **Failure Pareto (n=882 failed criteria):** `execution_slip` **36.4%** ·
  `constraint_unaddressed` **28.8%** · `constraint_misread` **14.4%** ·
  `degenerate_output` **9.8%** · `input_misread` **8.3%**. The top two
  (precision + coverage, **65.2%** combined) are the Gate E→T binding.
- **The panel redesign worked.** **Zero abstentions** across all 3 judges ×
  1,559 criteria (E07's Fable refused ~15%), Fleiss **κ = 0.883**, 0 excluded
  criteria, completeness satisfied on all cells. The verdict basis is trustworthy.
- **Fixing truncation *raised* `execution_slip`** (34.8% → 36.4%): the 1536 cap
  was masking execution errors — answers cut off before the model could finish
  botching them. Coverage (`constraint_unaddressed`) held rock-stable at 28.8%,
  and `degenerate_output` fell (12.0% → 9.8%) as budget artifacts dissolved.
- **Cross-family replication holds directionally:** the head set
  {execution_slip, constraint-tracking, constraint_misread} matches E07's Qwen
  ladder across a different family, scale, and reasoning mode (taxonomy changed,
  so directional not like-for-like).
- **Load-bearing caveat:** 95% of the Pareto's labels came from a single
  classifier (`claude-fable-5`); the human hand-check is **deferred**.

## Truncation repair (2026-07-15)

The census froze `max_tokens=1536` (§0.2 reasoning-off answer budget), but the
pipeline's own default is 8000 (with a comment that 4000 truncates complex
answers). 12/75 responses hit the 1536 cap (`finish_reason=length`) and were cut
off mid-answer, inflating `degenerate_output`. Fix
(`experiments/E08/repair_truncated.py`): re-draw only the 12 at **8192** tokens,
all other decode params frozen (reasoning off, temp 0.6, seed 20260715, bf16/fp16
pin), then re-grade / re-diagnose / re-aggregate via the normal resume path.

**Outcome — the repair cleanly separated artifact from real failure:**

- **9 of 12 finish cleanly at 8192** (CIF-009/011/012/016/026/031/049/060/072) —
  they were genuinely under-budgeted.
- **3 of 12 (CIF-047, CIF-054, CIF-056) truncate at 8192 too** — the model spews
  endless numbered lists (`1144. PT-2197 …`) or repeated lines
  (`-3%: Norman …`); non-looping by the mechanical detector but plainly
  degenerate. Correctly retained as `degenerate_output` (10 / 9 / 14 labels).

**Effect on the census:** criterion pass 42.78 → **43.43%**; failed criteria 892
→ **882**; `degenerate_output` 107 → **86**; and — the notable finding —
`execution_slip` 310 → **321** (34.8 → 36.4%): the tight cap had been *masking*
execution errors, because answers were truncated before the model finished
producing (and being caught on) its rendering mistakes. Coverage
(`constraint_unaddressed`) was unmoved (257 → 254, 28.8%), and κ rose
0.871 → 0.883. Backups and a manifest are in
`runs/E08-llama3-2-3b-cc75/repair-truncation/`.

## Experimental flaws, biases, and limitations

### Classifier provenance + deferred hand-check (the load-bearing caveat)
The root-cause Pareto — the input to Gate E→T — is **95.1% labeled by a single
classifier**: `claude-fable-5` diagnosed 839/882 labels, with `claude-opus-4-8`
as chain fallback (43 labels). Per §0.3 the **40-row manual label hand-check is
deferred**: the 3-judge panel is a stronger *verdict-reliability* control than a
40-row human spot-check, but it does **not** validate *cause labels*, which
remain human-unvalidated. The taxonomy also changed since E07 (coverage-family
merge), so E07's frozen classifier-prompt SHA no longer describes the classifier
in use. **Forward pointer:** if T01's primary result is null or ambiguous, the
hand-check is the recommended first follow-up (T1.6), not a blocker now.

### Sample-size + selection effects
Single candidate, single 75-prompt census (no sampling error vs the benchmark).
No within-family ladder, so scaling claims are cross-report only. Singleton
use-cases (n=1) excluded from interpretation. k=1 per cell — generation
stochasticity is unmeasured (the repaired 12 are fresh/continued draws at 8192).

### Decoding-mode caveat (now largely resolved)
The original `max_tokens=1536` truncated 12/75; the repair re-drew them at 8192,
leaving only 3 genuinely degenerate. Residual caveat: the census characterizes a
Meta-post-trained 3B at temperature 0.6, reasoning off — it does not generalize
to reasoning-on or greedy decoding.

### Judge biases
Self-preference is structurally absent (candidate is Meta-family; no judge is).
Verbosity/position/format biases are uncontrolled but bounded by the
near-perfect cross-provider agreement (κ=0.883). Human verdict validation
remains the open gate (60-row sample staged, unscored).

### Provider-side variance
The bf16/fp16 pin routed to Parasail (quantization reported `null` = native
precision) for both census and repair. Provider identity uncontrolled; no
timeouts/malformed responses observed.

### Cost / timing
Census wall-clock ≈ 1 h; repair ≈ a few minutes (12 regenerations + 36 re-grades
+ 12 re-diagnoses). **Token/dollar spend is not instrumented** (program-wide TODO
inherited from E07); pre-run estimate ~$30–60 for the 3-judge census, repair
< $1.
