## Configuration adjustments + justifications

1. **max_tokens 48000 → 64000** (pre-registered, spec §5): the 48k cap never
   bound on a healthy E05 response (59/60 stop; max healthy ≈27k tokens), so
   the raise is behaviorally identical treatment on the non-degenerate
   distribution; 64k sits under qwen-35b's 81,920 provider ceiling. For
   loops, no finite budget prevents truncation: the raise upgraded the
   diagnostic from "truncated at 48k" to "does the loop escape?"
2. **GENERATION_DEADLINE_S 2030 → 2700**: sized so a 64k run-to-cap is
   STORED (`finish_reason=length`, diagnosable) rather than deadline-aborted
   into an error.
3. **Rejected-draft capture** (2026-07-10, mid-experiment): generate's
   `on_reject` hook persists doomed drafts (empty completion, deadline
   abort) to `runs/<slug>/rejected/`: previously every retry discarded the
   looping text, the purest training-signal examples of the behavior.
4. **Loop-terminal policy** (2026-07-10, mid-experiment, user ruling): a
   zero-content draw whose reasoning/content loops is terminal on the first
   draw: stored flagged, graded all-FAIL mechanically (`loop_failure:`
   reason), keeping the prompt IN the aggregate as a failure. Non-looping
   empties remain retriable. **Heterogeneity note:** 220 responses came from
   the retry-up-to-5× policy, 5 from loop-terminal; the affected responses
   are conditioned differently on draw luck. All 220 old-policy responses
   are conditioned on "a non-empty draw occurred within 5 attempts":
   identical to E05's conditioning, so the E05↔E07 comparison is clean.

Verdict on E05 rec 3's question: degenerate_output on CIF-012 is a
**stochastic per-draw model behavior (~20%/draw), not a mechanical config

wants length-budget supervision. Taxonomy v2 should consider the split.

## Experimental flaws, biases, and limitations

### Sample-size + selection effects
Population run: zero sampling error vs the benchmark; remaining variance is
generation stochasticity (measured by the A/A cut) and judge/classifier
noise. Old-policy responses are conditioned on "a non-empty draw within 5
attempts" (same as E05); the 5 resume items are not directly comparable to
their own counterfactual under the old policy.

### Judge biases
Single canonical judge (Opus) for verdict basis; concurrence and
auto-verifiable proxies bound but do not eliminate judge idiosyncrasy.
Human census still the open gate. Fable refuses ~15% of cells consistently.

### Classifier biases
Root-cause labels remain LLM-assigned (blinded, evidence-first) and
human-unvalidated; judge_suspect hotspots (CIF-018 #18, CIF-058 #8) argue
at least two criteria are benchmark bugs inflating counts by up to 5 labels.

### Decoding-mode caveats
provider_sort=throughput pins routing preference, not provider identity;
provider drift is uncontrolled in both E05 and E07. Wilson intervals in the
stability artifact are supplementary only (criteria within a response are
correlated); the cluster bootstrap is authoritative.

### Anomaly hypotheses (not yet ruled in or out)
input_misread (11.2%, #4 at population scale) has a very wide CI
[3.7%, 20.2%]: heavily clustered in few disjoint-cut prompts; could be a
taxonomy-boundary artifact with constraint_misread. Negative-instruction
prompts appear over-represented among loop-fatal items (3 of 4 qwen-9b
retry-exhausted items); untested association, n tiny.
